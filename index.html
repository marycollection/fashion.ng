Our Story

MARY'S COLLECTIONS started as a small dream by Mary Emmanuel, a passionate Fashion Designer who believed every woman and man deserves to look executive, elegant and confident without breaking the bank.

What started with a single sewing machine, a few fabrics and a big vision has now grown into one of the most trusted fashion brands - known for quality, neat finishing, fast delivery and honest prices.

From beautiful gowns, to executive suits, to ready-to-wear collections, Mary personally supervises every piece.

She started as a Fashion Designer, sewing from a small shop, learning, growing, and building trust one customer at a time. Today, Mary's Collections delivers nationwide.
